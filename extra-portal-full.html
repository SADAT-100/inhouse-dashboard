      How each number is calculated

      Gross sales
      Product price plus shipping, incl. VAT, for every order line that was not canceled.
      Returns
      Refunded amounts incl. VAT, including partial refunds.
      Net sales
      Gross sales minus returns. Shown incl. VAT, and excl. VAT from the ledger.
      Commission
      Extra commission excl. VAT, minus commission given back on refunds.
      Gross profit
      Net sales excl. VAT minus commission, seller fees, subscription and ads. It is before the cost of the products.
      Net profit
      Gross profit minus unit cost × net units sold. Needs the cost of each product.
      Delivered
      Received and Closed together. Extra changes Received to Closed after some time.
      Pending payout
      Ledger lines not yet paid: Payable goes in the next transfer, Pending waits for the customer to receive the order.
      Period filter
      Order money follows the order date. Payouts, subscription and ads follow their own date.
