Open position

Online Channels Assistant

Based in Syria Full-time or part-time Amazon & Trendyol accounts Arabic + English

You will support our online channels team in running In-House and Back Comfort products on Amazon, Trendyol and our retail partners such as Al Rugaib, Home Centre, Centrepoint, Carrefour and Al-Saif Gallery, from building product pages to keeping prices, stock and customer messages under control.

What you will do

  • Create and update product listings: titles, bullet points, descriptions, images and keywords.
  • Keep prices and stock levels correct on every channel.
  • Follow up on orders, returns and customer messages.
  • Track competitors and suggest ways to improve our listings.
  • Prepare daily and weekly sales reports in Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Help the team run sponsored ads, campaigns and promotions.

What we look for

  • Native Arabic and good working English
  • Hands-on experience with an online marketplace or e-commerce store
  • Comfortable with Excel or Google Sheets
  • Organized, careful with details and reliable with deadlines
  • A laptop or desktop and a stable internet connection

Nice to have

  • Turkish, for the Trendyol seller panel
  • Experience with Amazon Seller Central
  • Canva or Photoshop for product images
  • Sponsored ads (PPC) experience
  • Tools like Helium 10 or Jungle Scout

Common questions

Do I need experience on Amazon specifically?

No. Experience on any marketplace or online store helps. Tell us what you have actually done and we will take it from there.

Which language should I use in the form?

Arabic or English, whichever you prefer. A few questions ask for a specific language, and they are clearly marked.

My internet connection is unstable. Will I lose my answers?

No. Your answers are saved on your device as you type. If the page closes, open it again on the same device and continue.

Who sees my information?

Only our hiring team. We use your information for this hiring process only.
